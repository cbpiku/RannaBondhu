# 🍽️ TasteAI - Your Smart Meal Planning Companion  
*(Or: RannaBondhu | AhaarMitra | BhateBhaat — the name’s cooking!)*

## 🤔 What is TasteAI?

**TasteAI** is your personalized AI-powered sous-chef that blends the warmth of homestyle cooking with the efficiency of intelligent automation.  
Tired of the **"Ki khabo aaj?"** dilemma? So are we.

TasteAI makes your kitchen smarter, planning your meals, managing your pantry, generating shopping lists, and guiding your cooking — all tailored to your lifestyle, preferences, and mood.

---

## 🔥 Why TasteAI?

We’ve all been there:
- You’re staring at the fridge with zero ideas 😵‍💫
- You bought dhaniya... again 😅
- Everyone’s asking “Aaj raat e ki hobe dinner?”  
TasteAI is built to end this everyday chaos and bring back the joy of food without the stress.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### ✨ Personalized Meal Planning
- Suggests recipes based on your preferences (veg/non-veg, dislikes, allergies)
- Considers what's already in your fridge/pantry
- Recommends meals you can actually cook — not just random Instagram-worthy ideas

### 🛒 Smart Grocery List
- Auto-generates shopping lists based on selected meals
- Syncs with grocery delivery APIs (e.g., Blinkit) for one-click shopping
- Prevents overbuying (no more 3 bunches of coriander)

### 🍳 Simple, Stress-Free Recipes
- Step-by-step instructions even a newbie can follow
- Voice-guided cooking and timers (planned)
- Recipes rooted in comfort — from **dal-chawal** to **kosha mangsho**

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Collaborative Cooking Mode *(Cook Collab)*
- Plan meals together with family or flatmates
- Assign tasks (e.g., “You chop, I sauté”)
- Share lists, sync meal prep, enjoy the joy of joto-shobai'r ranna

---

## 📦 Tech Stack (Prototype)

- **Python** – core logic for recipe suggestion and planning
- **CLI-based MVP** – command-line prototype for testing flows
- **Data Source** – hardcoded sample recipes (to be replaced by recipe DB or API)
- **Next Up**: Streamlit UI / Flask backend, grocery API integration, user auth
